DAY 1 · FRIDAY 23 OCTOBER

Land, and do almost nothing

Tokyo - the day that exists so she is rested before she drives

Morning

Land at Haneda 01:05. Taxi straight into town, about 35 minutes and 9,000 yen. Bed by half past two. Then sleep as late as you like.

Afternoon

A slow wander with nothing booked. Dashi Okume at Azabudai Hills - your friend's spot, where you blend your own dashi to take home.

Evening

Dinner with her friend.

Driving
None.
Public transport
Taxi, Haneda to Shinjuku, about 35 min. Metro after that.
Where you sleep
Tokyo, near Shinjuku. Tell the hotel you are arriving at 3am.

Two nights in the same Tokyo hotel so there is no packing on the 23rd.

DAY 2 · SATURDAY 24 OCTOBER

Into the Kiso valley

Tokyo to Tsumago - a train through the mountains, then a short drive

Morning

Breakfast, then the Azusa limited express from Shinjuku to Matsumoto. Two hours forty, and a beautiful ride in its own right.

Afternoon

Pick up the car at Matsumoto station. Drive south down the Kiso valley to the house in Nagiso, 3 km from Tsumago. There by mid-afternoon, in daylight, unhurried. Check-in from 16:00.

Evening

Drive 7 minutes into Tsumago and walk the village once the day-trippers have gone. The house has no dinner - ask the host to book a restaurant ahead.

Driving
Matsumoto to Nagiso. About 110 km, 2 hours. Her first drive on the left.
Public transport
Azusa express, Shinjuku to Matsumoto, 2h40. Reserve seats.
Where you sleep
tsumugi tei, Nagiso - booked. A renovated wooden house, 1 min from Nagiso station. Whole house, free parking.

DAY 3 · SUNDAY 25 OCTOBER

The Nakasendo walk

8 km downhill on the old highway between Kyoto and Tokyo

Morning

Drive to Tsumago and park. Bus up to Magome, then walk the Nakasendo back down to Tsumago and the car. Stone paving, cedar forest, two waterfalls. About three hours. Start after nine so the frost burns off.

Afternoon

Tsumago at your own pace. Or drive 40 minutes to the Atera gorge for an easy riverside walk in full colour.

Evening

Second night at the same house. Nothing to pack. Dinner booked ahead through the host.

Driving
Nagiso to Tsumago and back, 7 min each way. Atera gorge is optional, 40 min each way.
Public transport
Local bus, Tsumago to Magome, about 25 min.
Where you sleep
Same house, tsumugi tei.

A luggage service runs between the two villages, about 1,000 yen a bag, dropped by 11:30. You will not need it - your bags stay at the house - but it exists.

DAY 4 · MONDAY 26 OCTOBER

North to Kamikochi

One stop on the way, then the shuttle into the valley

Morning

Drive north up the Kiso valley. Stop at Narai-juku - the same idea as Tsumago with a third of the visitors. An hour is enough.

Afternoon

Over the pass to Hirayu. Park the car and take the shuttle in, 25 minutes. Private cars are banned in the valley, which is why it stays like this.

Evening

Walk out to Kappa Bridge as the day buses leave. Onsen, then dinner at the lodge.

Driving
Tsumago to Narai to Hirayu. About 140 km, 3 hours including the stop.
Public transport
Shuttle bus, Hirayu to Kamikochi, 25 min. Pack a small bag - the cases stay in the car.
Where you sleep
A hot spring lodge inside Kamikochi.

DAY 5 · TUESDAY 27 OCTOBER

The long one

20 km up the Azusa river, almost completely flat

Morning

Kappa Bridge to Myojin, then on to Tokusawa. A level riverside path with the Hotaka range above you the whole way.

Afternoon

On to Yokoo, then back. Six hours in total, no climbing and nothing technical. Just a long, satisfying day.

Evening

Onsen. Dinner. Bed early.

Driving
None. The car stays parked at Hirayu.
Public transport
None. Everything today is on foot from the lodge door.
Where you sleep
Same lodge in Kamikochi.

DAY 6 · WEDNESDAY 28 OCTOBER

The climb, or the spare

Dakesawa if the sky is good - otherwise hold the day back

Morning

Up to Dakesawa. 700 m of ascent, steep, but no chains and nothing exposed. Four to five hours return.

Afternoon

Back down slowly. If the weather is poor, do none of it and repeat whichever walk you liked best instead.

Evening

Onsen. Last dinner in the valley.

Driving
None.
Public transport
None.
Where you sleep
Same lodge in Kamikochi.

This is the weather day. Late October gives you one or two bad days in four - this is the one you can afford to lose.

DAY 7 · THURSDAY 29 OCTOBER

Dawn at the pond, then the hot springs

Out of the valley, up the ropeway, and then stop moving

Morning

Taisho Pond at first light, Yakedake reflected in it and frost on the grass. Then the shuttle out to Hirayu.

Afternoon

Shinhotaka ropeway to 2,156 m - the high alpine view without ice underfoot. Checked in at Okuhida by two.

Evening

The private open-air bath. Kaiseki dinner. Nothing else at all.

Driving
Hirayu to Shinhotaka to your ryokan. About 30 minutes in total.
Public transport
Shuttle bus out, 25 min. Then the ropeway - check the wind the night before.
Where you sleep
Okuhida Onsen. A room with its own open-air bath.

DAY 8 · FRIDAY 30 OCTOBER

Out of the mountains

The long way back, and one dinner at the end of it

Morning

A bath before breakfast. Then drive to Matsumoto and drop the car at the station.

Afternoon

Azusa express to Shinjuku, two hours forty. Cross to Ueno and check in. Rest - you have an early alarm.

Evening

Kagurazaka Sushi Yasaka. Edo-style sushi, 10,000 to 15,000 yen a head, booked well in advance. Just the two of you.

Driving
Okuhida to Matsumoto. About 60 km, 1h15. The last drive of the trip.
Public transport
Azusa express to Shinjuku, 2h40, then metro across to Ueno.
Where you sleep
Tokyo - Ueno or Nippori, right beside the airport train.

Home

One early alarm, and that is the whole day

Morning

Up at 05:20. Keisei Skyliner 05:58 from Ueno, at Narita around 06:45. Flight TR885 leaves at 08:50.

Afternoon

In the air.

Evening

Singapore at 15:30. Home in time for dinner.

None.

Your own bed.

Book this week

You are seven weeks out, in peak foliage season. This is the order that matters.

  1. A hot spring lodge inside Kamikochi26-28 Oct. Last month of the season and the hardest bed to get. Start here.
  2. Tsumago: tsumugi tei, Nagiso - booked24-26 Oct, 2 nights. Free cancellation until 24 Sep, in case a Tsumago inn has a room.
  3. Okuhida, private open-air bath29 Oct. Only one night now, so the room type has to be right.
  4. Sushi in Kagurazaka30 Oct, evening. Book direct or through a concierge service.
  5. Her International Driving PermitAA Singapore. Walk in, about S$20, valid in Japan.
  6. Your JAF licence translationTwo weeks by post. Start it now so you can share the driving.

The two Tokyo hotels and the rental car can wait a week. Nothing above can.

The driving licences

The one thing that can stop the trip at the rental desk

YOUR WIFE / SINGAPORE

She is the driver.

Singapore signed the 1949 convention, so a standard International Driving Permit from AA Singapore is fully valid in Japan. About S$20, walk in, done in a day.

YOU / FRANCE

A French IDP is worthless here.

France signed a different convention, so Japanese rental desks will refuse an IDP. What you need is an official Japanese translation of your licence from JAF - about 4,000 yen, two weeks by post.

Get her permit first. That alone unblocks the trip. Your translation is the backup that lets you share the wheel.

Carry the original licence together with the permit or translation at all times - the rental desk checks both, and so do the police.

Before you go

Small things that make a difference at this time of year

It will be cold

Kamikochi mornings run between -3 and +3 C in late October, with frost. Days reach about 12 C. Bring layers, gloves and a hat - not resort clothes.

Shoes, not boots

Everything here is on maintained trails. Good hiking shoes are enough. Nothing on this itinerary needs spikes.

Get an ETC card

Take the toll transponder card with the rental car. The expressways add up and it saves queuing at every barrier.

Carry cash

Mountain inns, the Kamikochi shuttle and the small post-town shops are often cash only.

Ask for a kashikiri bath

In Kamikochi the baths are communal and split by gender. Ask each lodge for a 貸切風呂 - a bath you book privately for 45 minutes.

Check the ropeway

Shinhotaka closes for wind or snow. Look at the forecast the night before and keep the day flexible.
